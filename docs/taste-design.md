## Direction and constraints

Direction: **Dressed as something else** (`borrowed-category`), seed **271028**. The borrowed category is a training field guide: large condensed titles, full photographs, factual service descriptions, a plain itinerary and a menu of support levels. On this page, OSSFIT's real sequence of assessment, assigned program and check-in becomes the structure of that field guide.

Palette and type are retained from the established brand, including Arabic glyph support. No new font or library is added. The original counter-object hero was redrawn once because its small product photograph, wide wordmark and empty lower screen conflict with the owner's explicitly requested image-overlay hero and compact phone layout. The resulting poster composition is adapted to that owner constraint: one large photographic field, readable HTML title and actions together. The original eight-to-twelve-point features layout was changed to photographed specimens for the four actual services; its flat fields use the four real local service photos without decorative specimen props. The two-plan pricing composition was changed to `pricing-menu-list` because three real packages exist. The three-part abstract process diagram was changed to `how-it-works-itinerary-on-a-slip` because four real application stages exist; no fictional weekdays or durations are added. The geometric footer is expressed by the real configured wordmark instead of inventing a new logo. These are content/brand constraints, not redraws to imitate the old layout.
